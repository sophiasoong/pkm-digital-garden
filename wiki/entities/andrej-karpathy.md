## Overview

Andrej Karpathy is an AI researcher and educator known for his work on deep learning and large language models. He co-founded OpenAI and served as the AI lead at Tesla before returning to the AI research world. He coined the term "vibe coding" and is widely known for making complex AI concepts accessible through his educational content and public writing.

In April 2026, Karpathy posted about using LLMs to build personal knowledge wikis, a tweet that went massively viral. He followed up the next day with a GitHub gist — an "idea file" — laying out the complete architecture, philosophy, and tooling of the pattern.
