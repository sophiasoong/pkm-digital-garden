## Concepts
- [[concepts/llm-wiki]] — LLM-maintained personal knowledge wiki; compiles knowledge at ingest time rather than re-deriving at query time.
- [[concepts/idea-file]] — Structured markdown describing a pattern for LLM agents to instantiate; "open ideas" rather than open source.
- [[concepts/rag-retrieval]] — Dominant alternative to LLM Wiki; retrieves from raw documents at query time with no accumulation.
- [[concepts/memex]] — Vannevar Bush's 1945 concept for personal associative knowledge stores; direct intellectual predecessor of the LLM Wiki.
