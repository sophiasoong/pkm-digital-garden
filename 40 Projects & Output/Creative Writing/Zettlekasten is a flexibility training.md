Zettlekasten is a flexibility training for our mind
Sometimes we need to focus on a single sentence
Other times we need to find connections among different ideas
Sometimes we need to dive deep into a single idea
Other times we need to relax and let ideas surface
We are free to flow in different states of mind in this non-linear workflow
