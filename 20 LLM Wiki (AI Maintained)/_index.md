## Concepts

- [[22 Concepts/hierarchy-utility-style]] — What an interface communicates, as three measurable dimensions; a measurement frame rather than a prescription.
- [[22 Concepts/liquid-glass]] — Apple's translucent material: refracts rather than blurs, and its defining property is also its failure mode.
- [[22 Concepts/ai-in-mathematics]] — Three partisan voices disagreeing about what automated reasoning is for: trajectory, complementarity, or verifiability.
- [[22 Concepts/status-economy-of-expertise]] — What a field rewards, and what happens when the difficulty it honors stops being scarce.
- [[22 Concepts/static-thinking-fallacy]] — Judging a developing technology by its present capability; a real error, stated so broadly it proves too much.
- [[22 Concepts/color-stimulation]] — Perceptual intensity of a color combination as intensity + hue distance + light-dark contrast, balanced like mixing-board sliders.
- [[22 Concepts/color-relativity]] — A color has no fixed appearance; the visual system pushes neighboring colors apart. No backdrop is neutral.
- [[22 Concepts/halfway-color-combinations]] — Hue pairs ~90° apart, between analogous and complementary; superseded by the stimulation model.
- [[22 Concepts/voluntary-discomfort]] — The deliberate practice of chosen hardship to maintain capacity and reduce dependencies.
- [[22 Concepts/stoicism]] — Parent philosophical framework; Roman Stoic tradition as it appears in ingested sources.
- [[22 Concepts/usability]] — How intuitive and easy to use a product is; defined via five aspects, operationalized via six design tactics.
- [[22 Concepts/dualism]] — Splitting mind from body, either by ignoring the body or despising it; both named "dangerous" by Lao Tzu.
- [[22 Concepts/mind-body]] — The alternative to dualism: mind and body as one, not two things in a hierarchy.
- [[22 Concepts/attentive-perception]] — Finding the vast/eternal within the small/momentary through close attention.
- [[22 Concepts/formalization]] — Machine-checkable proof as the answer to trust in automated mathematics; two worked cases, one of them a revision.
- [[22 Concepts/benchmark-critique]] — Why measuring human mathematical ability by benchmark corrupts what it measures — argued by someone who built one.
- [[22 Concepts/failure-as-method]] — Repeated failure as the mechanism rather than the cost; no eureka, and the helicopter warning.
- [[22 Concepts/research-bottleneck-shift]] — When a tool speeds some stages and not others, the constraint moves: proof indigestion and the seed-corn problem.
- [[22 Concepts/six-essentials-of-mathematics]] — Numbers, algebra, geometry, probability, analysis, dynamics — one mathematician's frame for the whole subject.
