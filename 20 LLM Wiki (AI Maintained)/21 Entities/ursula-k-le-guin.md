## Overview

Ursula K. Le Guin is the translator and commentator behind this edition of the Tao Te Ching. This wiki's only source on her is a single-paragraph excerpt of her Chapter 80 commentary, so this page is a stub — it covers only her role and voice in this specific passage, not her broader biography or body of work, which no ingested source has yet addressed.

## Key Facts

- Author of this rendition of the Tao Te Ching, including prose commentary on individual chapters (e.g., Chapter 80, "Freedom"). [[23 Sources/le-guin-tao-te-ching-1997]]
- In her Chapter 80 commentary, presents Lao Tzu's teaching that both materialistic and religious dualism are dangerous, and renders his alternative in plain, direct language: "live in your body, you are your body." [[23 Sources/le-guin-tao-te-ching-1997]]
