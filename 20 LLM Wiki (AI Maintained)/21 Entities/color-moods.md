## Overview

A color tool at colormoods.co, co-created by [[21 Entities/ruxandra-duru]], presented in her Google Design article as the practical implementation of the stimulation model she describes there. Where the article explains that stimulation rises with overall intensity, hue distance and light-dark contrast, the tool is the place those sliders are actually operated.

Its origin is documented in this wiki rather than inferred: "Rethinking Color Theory" lists "Two-Color Combinations: A Toolkit" among its additional resources with the note that this is where Color Moods came from [[23 Sources/duru-rethinking-color-theory-2026]]. That makes the tool the middle term of a three-stage progression — the 2021 pair catalogue, the toolkit and tool, then the general framework.

Little else about the tool is established from the ingested sources: no feature description, interface account or weighting method is given. The pages here deliberately stop short of that rather than guess.
