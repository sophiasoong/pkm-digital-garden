## Overview

The Nielsen Norman Group (NN/g) is cited in this wiki as the originating source for a five-aspect definition of usability, adopted by Material Design as the conceptual foundation for its own usability guidance. No source ingested so far describes NN/g directly (its history, founders, or broader body of work) — everything known here is filtered through Material Design's citation of it.

## Key Facts

- Defines usability through five aspects: efficiency, errors, learnability, memorability, satisfaction. [[23 Sources/material-design-usability-2026]]
  - Efficiency: users can efficiently complete tasks and goals.
  - Errors: design reduces the likelihood of mistakes and makes errors easy to correct.
  - Learnability: new users learn to complete tasks easily, even on first use.
  - Memorability: returning users remember how to use the product.
  - Satisfaction: users are satisfied with the experience.
