## Overview

William Blake is the author of "Auguries of Innocence," represented in this wiki so far only by its opening quatrain. This page is necessarily a stub — it reflects four lines of one poem, not his broader body of poetry, art, or philosophy.

## Key Facts

- Wrote "Auguries of Innocence," which opens by pairing the vast/eternal with the small/momentary: a world in a grain of sand, heaven in a wild flower, infinity in a hand, eternity in an hour. [[23 Sources/blake-auguries-of-innocence-1803]]
- The poem is believed written around 1803 but was not published until 1863, posthumously. [[23 Sources/blake-auguries-of-innocence-1803]]
