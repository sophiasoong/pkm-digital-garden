What is overthinking?
Mostly an attempt to achieve certainty about uncontrollable outcomes
e.g. Information overload to minimize the risk of our decisions and maximize the chance for success

Enslaved Epictetus' reality check: some things are up to us, most things are not.
Marcus Aurelius' reality check: where is this happening? 

### Question 1: Can I do anything about this **right now**?

Yes: Will I take that action? Yes and Go!
No: Stop thinking until I'm ready to take actions
Because worry won't solve problems, actions will

Illusion between worrying about something vs. working on something?
Worry feels like we're doing something to solve the problem, but we feel more powerless when we worry without taking actions

### Question 2: What is it? (temporal sorting)

We feel past regret, present difficulty, and future anxiety as if they all happen simultaneously and need our attention urgently
### What happened
We **cannot** change it (what I should have done instead), because it already existed in the past
We **can** learn from it: 
1) Reframe our character, narrative, judgment, response
2) Move forward within our control

### What might happen
Hypothetical scenario, because it hasn't occurred and might never occur
We **cannot** control whether it arrive
We **can** prepare for likely scenarios, for best or for worst

### What’s happening?
The only authority we have is in the present, the only place where actions can influence outcomes
We cannot control the situation
We can control our response to the situation

### Question 3: Am I trying to 1) solve a problem or 2) feel certain about an outcome?

- Problem-solving process:
1.  Identify variables you can control
2. Take action on those variables
3. Assess results
4. Adjust approach
Can validate efficiency and impact

- Certainty about outcomes you cannot control is impossible:
	- How someone will respond
	- How the opportunity will work out
	- How the long-term choices will lead to
