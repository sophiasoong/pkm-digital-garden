💡 The Core Idea  
If every subject matter is a building, philosophy is its foundation. 
The foundation is built by 3 questions / pillars:
1. What is "Real"? Study of Being (Ontology): same as / different from...?
2. How do we "know"? Study of Knowledge (Epistemology): logical reasoning and evidence?
3. What is "important"? Study of Value (Axiology): aesthetics & ethics
For example
To study AI, we need to clarify what is AI? What's the difference between AI / human? How do we learn and judge it? What is a better future with AI to make it beautiful and ethical?
