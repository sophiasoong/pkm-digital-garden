UI communicates in 3 areas to answer questions that users might have:

* Hierarchy: Where to focus?
* Perceived Utility: What does it do? How to use it?
* Style: What does it make me feel? Who made it, and who is it for?

The design questions following the 3 areas:

* Hierarchy:
   * Question: how effectively does it guide users' gaze to the most important and useful feature?
   * Variables: size, placement, contrast, motion
   * Efficiency vs. inefficiency in hierarchy: use of space; how informative the design is
* Perceived Utility:
   * Question: how effectively does it match users' mental model on how to use the product?
   * Variables: obvious, clear
   * Design System helps bridge designers and users' mental models
* Style:
   * Question: how the overall design makes users feel?
   * Variables: modern, clean, positive, vibe, creative, personality, playful, friendly
   * Exciting vs. Draining feedback
