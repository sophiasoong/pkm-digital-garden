### Findings

**1. No done-marker (resolved).** Four of six notes were fully ingested but sat in the
inbox indistinguishable from the two that were not. An inbox without a processed signal
stops functioning as a queue and becomes an archive whose state must be re-derived by
grepping the wiki every session.

**2. Genre error in `Fleeting Notes/` (resolved).** The folder held two items with
opposite destinations: `Auguries of Innocence.md` is transcribed external material
(Blake's words, wiki-bound) while `UI communicates in 3 areas.md` is original synthesis
(Zettelkasten-bound). Two sub-folders split by provenance, one by something else.

**3. No traceability from wiki source pages to inbox originals (partly resolved).**
Zero inbound wikilinks to any inbox note from outside the folder. For `Usability.md` and
`What does your UI say to your users?.md` the wiki page is a *summary* — the staged clip
is the only verbatim record, and nothing connected them. Now linked from the inbox side
via `wiki_source:`; the reciprocal link from each wiki source page is still absent.

**4. Frontmatter was all-or-nothing (resolved).** The three Web Clips carried full
Obsidian Clipper frontmatter; the three hand-written notes had none, not even a capture
date. The only timestamp on the Blake quatrain was filesystem mtime, which any sync or
edit rewrites.

**5. Naming drift (resolved).** `ursula_le_guin_tao_te_ching.md` was snake_case against
Title Case everywhere else.

**6. `00 Meta/CLAUDE.md` stale in three ways (one resolved).**
- `35 Bridge/` was documented with three sub-folders and did not exist on disk — the
  mechanism where the two zones meet was unbuilt. `Queries/` now exists (this file is
  its first entry); `Evergreen/` and `MOCs/` remain documented but absent.
- Legacy `raw/` and `wiki/` are described as "still active"; both are deleted.
- The inbox map lists `PDFs & EPUBs/` and `Fleeting/`; disk has `Book Quotes/` and
  `Fleeting Notes/`.
- Separately, the ownership table marks `40 Projects & Output/` as agent-read-only,
  which the Design notes written there already contradict.

### Changes applied 2026-10-01

- `status:` added to all 6 notes (`ingested` | `unprocessed`), with `wiki_source:`
  back-pointers on the four ingested ones.
- `captured:` added to the 3 hand-written notes. Not added to the Web Clips — their
  Clipper `created:` field already serves that role, and duplicating it would create
  two fields to keep in sync.
- `Auguries of Innocence.md` moved `Fleeting Notes/` → `Book Quotes/`.
- `ursula_le_guin_tao_te_ching.md` renamed to `Tao Te Ching — Ch. 80 Freedom.md`.
- `35 Bridge/Queries/` created.

No external wikilinks pointed at either moved file, so nothing broke. Obsidian resolves
links by filename rather than path, so the move alone would have been safe regardless.

### Recommendation: permanent records, existing name

`10 Inbox & Raw/` should keep its files permanently and keep its current name.

The wiki is a *summary* layer. Delete a staged clip and the verbatim original is gone,
while the wiki page that cites it remains — which quietly breaks the no-hallucination
rule, since the citation becomes unverifiable. Link rot makes this concrete rather than
theoretical: `m3.material.io` is client-rendered and already resists automated
re-fetching, so the local clip is the durable copy. Storage cost is negligible.

The queue function that a permanent archive would otherwise lose is now carried by
`status:` instead of by the folder emptying. That separation is the real fix — stage
lives in the field, provenance lives in the folder.

The name needs no change because it already describes both roles: *Inbox* is the queue,
*Raw* is the archive. It reads as a contradiction only while the queue is invisible.
